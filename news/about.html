About the DigiByte Wallet Newsroom

Official updates, original release reporting and practical explanations about DigiByte Wallet and its related open-source development.

Who publishes this newsroom

DigiByte Wallet is the publisher of this newsroom. It is operated as a first-party publication for the DigiByte Wallet software project—not as an independent third-party news outlet or an official publication of the DigiByte blockchain network.

We cover wallet releases, development milestones, security-related maintenance and material changes affecting our software. The site's published privacy policy identifies Sigma Systems Inc. in its copyright notice.

Contact the publisher

For questions, corrections, technical clarifications and press inquiries, contact digibytewalletorg@gmail.com.

Editorial standards

We distinguish released software from previews or planned features, link to primary technical sources where available, and avoid treating automated testing as a third-party security audit. Release information is checked against published packages, source changes or official distribution listings before describing a feature as available.

Articles carry publication dates and identify the project or editorial author responsible for the information.

Corrections and updates

We welcome factual corrections. Email the article URL and the issue to our contact address. Material corrections are made on the original page, with an update note when warranted. We do not silently republish old articles as new simply to change their date.

Editorial independence and advertising

This newsroom publishes first-party project news and therefore has a direct interest in the software it covers. Our website or app may display advertising to support development. Advertising does not turn release previews into completed releases, and any paid or sponsored editorial content will be clearly labeled.

Sources and scope

We prioritize publicly verifiable project resources such as the wallet's source code, package registries, version histories and official app-store listings. DigiByte is a decentralized blockchain; publishing DigiByte Wallet news does not imply ownership or control of that network.

For the latest announcements, visit DigiByte Wallet News. This page describes our editorial approach and does not provide personalized investment advice.
